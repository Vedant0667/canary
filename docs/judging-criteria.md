‏ Scoring Guidance
‏	•	35–40: Product-ready — scalable, repeatable, and commercially viable
‏	•	28–34: Strong — good structure, minor gaps in standardization or scaling
‏	•	20–27: Moderate — still too custom or unclear path to scale
‏	•	<20: Weak — prototype-only, not productizable
